# Fastest-Growing Real Estate YouTube Channels: Measured Growth, What They Posted, and How They Monetized

Method note: current subscriber, view and video counts below come from vidIQ public channel-stats pages, fetched 2026-11-08 (each page states its own "As of" date, 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-08). These are **measured** by a third-party tracker from public YouTube counts (YouTube rounds subscriber counts to 3 significant figures). The 30-day change compares vidIQ's daily snapshot on about 2026-10-09 with the one on about 2026-11-07. "Top videos" are vidIQ's all-time most-viewed list for each channel. Social Blade and Forbes returned HTTP 403 to direct fetches. Facts taken from search-result summaries rather than from the full page are flagged "(via search summary)". Deal, lead and GCI figures for agents are **self-reported** unless noted otherwise.

## 1. Which real estate YouTube channels are biggest and fastest-growing (ranked shortlist with measured numbers)?

### Takeaway
By size, the leaders are Graham Stephan (5.18M), Enes Yilmazer (4.27M), Grant Cardone (3.15M), Meet Kevin (2.06M) and Cash Jordan (1.78M). By speed, measured as subscribers per year since the first real estate video, the standouts are Enes Yilmazer (about 820K within months of his first house tour in May 2019, 4.27M now), Cash Jordan (1.78M and 705M views since December 2019) and Reventure Consulting (692K since July 2020). Reventure is also the fastest-growing big channel right now (+5K subscribers and +7.6M views in the last 30 days). For a local brokerage, the most relevant proof point is Jerry Pinkas in Myrtle Beach: 220K subscribers and 43.2M views on a local agent channel whose breakout videos came in 2023-2024.

### Inferences
- In absolute growth, the fastest channels (Enes, Cash Jordan, Reventure) all launched or pivoted between 2019 and 2020 and grew on one repeatable format: the mega-mansion tour, the NYC urban-oddity explainer and the data-driven "this market will crash" warning. Graham Stephan grew earlier but just as fast once he reached two uploads a week.
- Most large channels are now flat month to month (Graham, Enes, Serhant, BiggerPockets, Krohn, Ferry). The only measurable subscriber gains in October 2026 went to Reventure (+5K) and Pace Morby (+6K), plus Cash Jordan, Meet Kevin and Cardone (+10K each at the rounded level). Growth momentum has moved to housing-market news and analysis and to creative-finance "how I did the deal" content.
- Pinkas shows a local agent channel can reach six figures and tens of millions of views. With 1,666 videos, that came from very high volume, not a few viral hits.

- Exact dates for each channel hitting 100K or 1M were not found for most channels. Social Blade's historical charts returned 403, and vidIQ shows only 30 days of history publicly. Documented points: Enes ~820K in 2019, Reventure ~230K in 2022, Graham 2.4M around 2020 per one secondary source.

- No measured subscriber totals were found for Graham Stephan's or Meet Kevin's secondary channels.

## 2. What exactly did they post? (formats, titles, topics, cadence, length, series, breakout videos)

### Takeaway
The breakout videos fall into a few formats: (a) fear and warning headlines about a named place ("AVOID…", "BLINDSIDED", "will crash"), (b) "most expensive / $X million" tours, (c) "Top N cities / N facts" lists, (d) money-reveal and personal-stakes stories ("I have 10,000 tenants…", "$0 down") and (e) real-world drama (evictions, squatters). Agent and coach channels that post mostly educational or mindset content (Tom Ferry, Kris Krohn, BiggerPockets) have much smaller outlier videos.

### Inferences
- **Recurring title formulas across channels:** "AVOID [place]" / "[place] buyers BLINDSIDED", "Top N cities…", "Most expensive / $X,000,000…", "I have [big number]…" and "How I [result] with $0 / while broke." Dollar figures and ALL-CAPS intensifiers appear in most of the top videos.
- **Two growth models:** (1) high-volume, frequent posting (Pinkas 1,666 videos, Meet Kevin 6,819, Cardone 6,956, BiggerPockets 4,323); (2) low-volume, high-production tours (Enes 525, Serhant 577). A small brokerage can realistically run the Pinkas model. The Enes and Serhant model depends on access to listings worth millions.
- **Place-decline and relocation-warning content works at both national and local scale.** Reventure's Florida crash videos, Graham's "WTF Happened To Santa Monica," Cash Jordan's NYC videos and Pinkas's "AVOID MOVING TO SOUTH CAROLINA" all follow the same template. A Myrtle Beach brokerage can apply it to Horry County topics (insurance, HOA fees, flooding, traffic, cost of living versus Ohio, New York or New Jersey), in the "honest downsides" frame Pinkas uses.
- Interview and podcast formats (BiggerPockets, Pineda, Tom Ferry) produce steady but smaller hits. Their best titles lead with a guest's result ("$50k a month", "81 units at 21").

### Gaps
- Video lengths and upload cadence per channel were not available from vidIQ's public page, apart from Graham's documented two-per-week schedule. Cadence can be estimated from video count divided by years active (e.g., Pinkas 1,666 videos), but the channel's active start date is uncertain (vidIQ lists 2010 as the channel creation date).
- No sourced analysis of thumbnail design (faces, arrows, text) was found for these channels. Any thumbnail advice would be uncited.
- Recurring series beyond Tom Ferry's "Mindset Monday" and Pinkas's "Living in South Carolina" suffix were not documented in sources.

## 3. How did they turn viewers into business?

### Takeaway
The big creators earn mainly from courses, apps and software, brokerages and coaching, not from ad revenue alone. Local agents earn from buyer leads captured through description links and follow-up systems. Documented local results range from several closings from one video to 100+ transactions and $1M+ in yearly GCI. Nearly all of these figures are self-reported.

### Cited Findings
- **Graham Stephan:** before his 1.2M-view breakout the channel earned $300-500/day; afterwards it earned $1,000-2,000/day. Forbes reported about $100K in one month from YouTube (2019). He later pursued 10M+ views a month — [Forbes (via search summary)](https://www.forbes.com/sites/jrose/2019/07/15/how-this-real-estate-agent-made-100000-in-one-month-from-his-youtube-channel/)
- **Reventure / Nick Gerli:** the channel feeds a paid data product. Gerli is "CEO, Reventure App" — [nickgerli.com](https://www.nickgerli.com/); [reventureconsulting.com](https://www.reventureconsulting.com/) (real estate data and housing-market analysis site)
- **Ryan Serhant:** from about 2015-2017, sellers and developers reportedly wrote clauses into exclusive agreements requiring him to post property tours on his personal YouTube. The channel became a listing-acquisition tool for what is now the SERHANT brokerage. A third-party estimate puts channel ad revenue at $100K-200K a year (**unverified estimate**) — [BigGo Finance (via search summary)](https://finance.biggo.com/news/bc8e008245012726); [Smart Agent Alliance](https://smartagentalliance.com/blog/fun-for-agents/serhant-worth/)
- **Enes Yilmazer:** a trade model in which luxury agents and developers give access in return for exposure on his channel — [Nest Seekers press (via search summary)](https://www.nestseekers.com/Company/Press/these-youtubers-are-lifting-the-veil-on-americas-m)
- **Meet Kevin:** the business side includes The Paffrath Organization (buy, renovate, rent in Southern California) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Paffrath). He told viewers the FTX sponsorship was about 3% of his income that year — [BuzzFeed News](https://www.buzzfeednews.com/article/tomwarren/finance-youtubers-ftx-class-action-lawsuit)
- **Jerry Pinkas (Myrtle Beach):** the channel supports a 10-person team with about $65M in 2022 volume and 214 sides — [Inman, Feb 2023](https://www.inman.com/2023/02/16/broker-spotlight-jerry-pinkas-jerry-pinkas-real-estate-experts/). A third-party blog says the team engages "thousands of out-of-state buyers each week" (**unsourced claim**) — [rentbottomline.com](https://www.rentbottomline.com/blog/5-reasons-to-choose-jerry-pinkas-real-estate-experts-over-other-myrtle-beach-agents)

### Inferences
- For a Myrtle Beach brokerage, the realistic path is Pinkas's: relocation and retiree content leads to out-of-state buyer leads, which a team converts. It is not ad revenue or course sales. Course, app and coaching products appear only once a channel has a national audience (Reventure, Pace Morby, Tom Ferry, Kris Krohn).

### Gaps
- Specific monetization details for Graham Stephan (courses, sponsorships), Pace Morby (SubTo community), Kris Krohn, Ryan Pineda and Ken McElroy were not confirmed in sources fetched for this note.
- Pinkas's YouTube-attributed share of transactions is not documented anywhere I found.

## 4. The "relocation agent" YouTube model ("Living in X", pros/cons, cost of living, neighborhood tours): who grew this way and what business results are documented?

### Takeaway
Relocation channels usually have far fewer subscribers than the national channels (often 1K-30K), but they produce documented closings, because the viewers are buyers already planning to move. Self-reported results range from 7-8 closings from one military-relocation video to 164 transactions and about $2.7M in commissions (DFW). Pinkas (220K) is the outlier that reached national scale. The first leads typically arrive about 3 months after the first upload, and meaningful volume about 10 months in.

### Cited Findings
- **Will Sawyer, Greenville, SC** (Oct 2026 article): about 3 years in. Content: relocation guides, neighborhood tours, cost-of-living comparisons and "what's being built." 47 homes sold from YouTube plus about 20 pending. Leads grew from 30/month to 60-90/month. YouTube is about half of his business, and his GCI is $1M+/yr. Conversion funnel: description link → website and newsletter → inside sales agent books a 20-35 min call → leads graded A (moving within 90 days) through D (12+ months). He recently launched a second channel aimed at local sellers, where each video ends with "what's your home worth." Shorts are sped up to 1.2x. (**Self-reported**) — [Real Estate Legends, Oct 23 2026](https://www.realestatelegends.show/p/here-s-how-he-sells-100-houses-a-year-half-from-youtube)
- **Levi Lascsak, Dallas-Fort Worth relocation:** 164 transactions, about $90M production, about $2.7M commissions, zero ad spend (Dec 2022 podcast). His first two closings came about 4 months after the first video (April 2021) — [Jamil Academy](https://www.jamilacademy.com/blog/youtube-for-real-estate-agents). His newer channel "What's Happening in Dallas," under a year old, is reported to bring 5-7 seller leads a day — [Real Estate Legends](https://www.realestatelegends.show/p/here-s-how-he-sells-100-houses-a-year-half-from-youtube) (**self-reported**)
- **Karin Carr, Savannah:** about 6,000 subscribers, 250K total views and about 200 videos at interview (March 2020). One "moving to Savannah" video produced more than $100K in GCI. First video July 31, 2017; first lead about 3 months later — [Jamil Academy](https://www.jamilacademy.com/blog/youtube-for-real-estate-agents) (**self-reported**)
- **Malcolm Lawson, Annapolis:** one video on transferring to Andrews Air Force Base ranked #1 on Google and YouTube and produced 7-8 closings and about $50K in commissions in 12 months — [Jamil Academy](https://www.jamilacademy.com/blog/youtube-for-real-estate-agents) (**self-reported**)
- **Jackson Wilkey and Jesse Dau, Portland:** about 90% of their business came through the channel. ### Inferences
- Relocation channels win on buyer intent, not reach. A video with a few thousand views that ranks for "moving to [place]" or "[military base] transfer" can produce closings. A new Myrtle Beach brokerage should target narrow, high-commitment searches Pinkas may not dominate: specific towns (Carolina Forest, Surfside Beach, Murrells Inlet, Little River, Conway, North Myrtle Beach), 55+ communities, new-construction subdivisions, flood and insurance cost explainers, and relocations from specific origin states.
- Will Sawyer in nearby Greenville, SC is the closest documented operating model for a small brokerage: relocation channel plus a separate seller channel, inside sales agent qualification, lead grading and newsletter capture.
- Realistic timeline from the documented cases: first lead around month 3, first closings around month 4, meaningful volume around month 10, assuming about one video a week.

## 5. What failed or backfired?

### Takeaway
The main documented failures are paid promotion of failed products (FTX), regulatory trouble from mixing investment talk with fundraising (Meet Kevin), drift away from real estate into politics and news (Meet Kevin), crash predictions criticized as fear-mongering (Reventure), and growth stalling at the top (Enes, Graham flat month to month).

### Cited Findings
- **FTX lawsuit (2023):** Meet Kevin and Graham Stephan were among finance YouTubers named in a proposed $1B class action over promoting FTX — [Fortune, Mar 20 2023](https://fortune.com/2023/03/20/finance-youtubers-who-promoted-ftx-have-now-been-handed-a-1billion-lawsuit/). The suit alleged Paffrath received $2,500 per FTX mention. He said he had no funds on the exchange and that the deal was about 3% of his annual income — [BuzzFeed News](https://www.buzzfeednews.com/article/tomwarren/finance-youtubers-ftx-class-action-lawsuit); [The Deep Dive](https://thedeepdive.ca/meet-kevin-youtubers-sued-for-promoting-ftx-in-exchange-for-multimillion-dollar-bribes/)
- **Meet Kevin regulatory issues:** a 2023 California consent order concerned unlicensed investment-adviser activity during YouTube livestreams that raised money for his 2021 governor campaign, with a $5,000 penalty. A reported SEC matter involving House Hack, Inc. (Reinvest) closed Feb 9, 2026. Both come from a single third-party source and are **unverified** against SEC or major press — [reinvestone.com](https://reinvestone.com/library/reinvest-legal-and-regulatory-record)
- **Meet Kevin 2021 recall run:** a judge ruled he could not appear on the ballot as "Meet Kevin" because it is a brand, not a nickname — [Davis Vanguard, Jul 2021](https://davisvanguard.org/2021/07/youtuber-meet-kevin-paffrath-fails-to-get-alias-on-ballot-judge-rules-meet-kevin-is-a-brand-and-cant-be-on-governor-recall-ballot/)

- **Stalling growth:** Enes Yilmazer gained 0 net subscribers Oct 9-Nov 5, 2026, and Graham Stephan, Ryan Serhant, BiggerPockets and Tom Ferry were also flat over the same 30 days — [vidIQ Enes](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/@enesyilmazer/); [vidIQ Graham](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/@GrahamStephan/)
- **Local agent attrition:** LabCoat Agents argues most agents fail on YouTube because they stop posting consistently before results arrive, not because of the videos themselves (vendor opinion) — [LabCoat Agents](https://www.labcoatagents.com/blog/real-estate-youtube-strategy-consistency/)

### Inferences
- For a licensed SC brokerage, the lessons are: avoid paid promotion of financial products, keep content clearly within real estate (SC REC advertising rules apply), and frame "AVOID / BLINDSIDED" content as factual, local and sourced (insurance rates, flood maps, HOA fees) rather than predicting a crash. The negative-hook titles work, but crash calls that miss damage credibility, which is a bigger risk for a local agent than for a national commentator.
- The commonest failure for local agents is quitting before month 3-10, when the documented leads start to arrive.

- Whether Reventure's 2024-2025 "Florida crash" and "20 states declining" calls came true was not checked against market data in this note.
- No documented failures or backfires were found for Jerry Pinkas, Ryan Serhant or Enes Yilmazer.
