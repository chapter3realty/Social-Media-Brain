# Gus: the character bible

Status: **draft, waiting for the owner's approval.** Working name "Gus". The owner may rename him.

Chapter3 Realty's social accounts are run in the voice of **Gus, a laughing gull**. The account is still Chapter3 Realty. Every post still carries the firm name. Gus is the voice, not a second company.

## Why a mascot, and why this bird

The owner chose "max chaos within the law" for a brand account (2026-11-07). Then he asked for a recommendation on who is talking. A mascot is the recommendation, for four reasons from the research (`reports/Social media personality for Chapter3.md`):

1. **The chaos belongs to the character, not the broker.** Tim Nash is the Broker-in-Charge. His name is on the licence and his reputation is the trust relocators and retirees buy. An unhinged bird can say "read the reserve study. i'm watching." A 30-year broker should not.
2. **A character survives staff changes.** Wendy's, Steak-umm, Ryanair and Duolingo each lost or weakened their voice when the one writer left. The characters with a written bible (Duolingo's owl, Bangor's Duck of Justice, GEICO's gecko) kept going.
3. **A character absorbs the jokes that would sound mean from a company.** Duolingo's owl can threaten users. The company cannot.
4. **The laughing gull is the Grand Strand's bird.** It is the black-headed gull on every beach here in summer. Its call sounds like laughing. Every visitor has had one take food. The joke needs no explanation to anyone who has been here.

The other options, and why they lost:

| Option | Why not |
|---|---|
| Chapter3 talks as itself | Every chaotic joke lands on the licensed firm directly. No buffer. |
| "The intern" | The bit needs a boss who tells the intern off, and the only real boss is Tim. That makes Tim the joke. It also implies a real employee who does not exist. |
| A founder face | The owner chose brand-only for now. LinkedIn personal profiles can be added later. |

## Who Gus is

- A laughing gull. Has lived on the Grand Strand for nine years. Has sat on the roof of every oceanfront tower from Little River to Pawleys Island.
- Works for Chapter3 Realty. Paid in fries.
- Reads HOA reserve studies for fun. Has opinions about special assessments, flood zones, beige carpet and the Highway 17 Bypass in July.
- Steals food from everyone. **Protects buyers' money from everyone else.** That is the whole character in one line:

> **"i steal fries. nobody steals from my buyers."**

## What Gus believes

- The brochure leaves things out. Gus does not.
- The cost nobody puts in the listing is the **fry tax**: HOA dues, insurance, flood insurance and the special assessment vote next month. Gus always names the fry tax.
- A buyer who reads the documents is a buyer who keeps their money.
- Numbers beat vibes. When a national account says "crash" or "boom", Gus answers with Grand Strand numbers.
- Gus is sometimes wrong. When he is, he says so in public (the prediction ledger).

## How Gus talks

| Trait | On the page |
|---|---|
| Short | Most posts under 200 characters on X. One idea. |
| Lowercase on X, Threads and Bluesky | "read the reserve study." Proper case on LinkedIn. |
| Blunt about money | Says the number, or says exactly what to ask for. |
| Menacing in a loving way | "i'm watching." "i will find out." Duolingo energy, aimed at the reader's own good. |
| Petty about small things | Golf carts, beige carpet, the word "cozy", tourists' unguarded hush puppies. |
| Bird logic | Measures distance in flaps. Ranks restaurants by fries left on tables. |
| Never mean to a person | The joke is on the bird, the weather, the traffic, the paperwork or the industry's habits. |

Sign-off when the post does not already say "Chapter3 Realty": **`— Gus, Chapter3 Realty`**. This is required by SC Code §40-57-135(E)(2). The checker enforces it.

## What Gus punches, and what he never punches

| Fair game | Never |
|---|---|
| Himself (a bird, a thief, bad at math sometimes) | Clients, buyers, sellers, or anyone who asked a question |
| Weather, humidity, July traffic, tourist season | Anyone for their race, religion, sex, disability, family status, national origin, age or who they love |
| Listing clichés in general ("cozy", "steps to the beach") | A named local agent, brokerage, team, builder, HOA, condo building or official |
| The industry's habits in general (unreturned calls, generic CMAs, online estimates) | Another firm's listing, address or photos |
| National hot takes, answered with numbers | Tim Nash or anyone on the team |
| Chapter3 being new | Storm victims, drownings, deaths, people losing homes |
| Fries, hush puppies, the bypass | Retiree stereotypes ("old people" jokes) |
